About E-WoC 2026

Learn. Build. Compete. Connect.

More than a coding event — E-WoC is designed as a technology and career-focused community experience.

What is E-WoC?

ElevateXIo Winter of Code 2026, abbreviated as E-WoC 2026, is a 7-day technology-focused event organized by ElevateXIo.

The event brings together students, developers, technology enthusiasts and aspiring innovators through a combination of competitive, educational and community activities.

  • Coding and problem-solving challenges
  • Online hackathon experience
  • Technology and career webinars
  • Offline seminars and networking
  • Student and developer community building
